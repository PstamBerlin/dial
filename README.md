# Dial

A photo editor that runs in your browser. Your photos never leave your device.

Live at https://dial.peterstam.eu. Made in Berlin by Peter Stam.

Fonts: Instrument Serif and Instrument Sans (SIL Open Font License, `fonts/OFL.txt`). Skin finder: Google MediaPipe (Apache License 2.0, `vendor/mediapipe/NOTICE.txt`).
